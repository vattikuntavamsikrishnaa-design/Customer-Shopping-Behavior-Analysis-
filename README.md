**Project Highlights**

Analyzed 3,900 customer purchase records to identify shopping patterns, customer segments, and product preferences.

Used Python for data cleaning, missing-value handling, feature engineering, and data preparation.

Used SQL to analyze revenue, customer loyalty, discounts, subscriptions, ratings, and purchase behavior.

Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize key customer shopping trends and business insights.

Developed business recommendations around subscriptions, loyalty programs, discount strategies, product positioning, and targeted marketing.
